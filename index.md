# Privacy Policy for Japanese Word Match

**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Japanese Word Match ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Your privacy is our top priority.

## 1. Our Core Philosophy: We Collect Nothing
We believe your data belongs to you. The App is designed to be a completely offline, educational experience. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. There are no user accounts, no registration, and no in-app purchases.

## 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
To function properly, the App may store the following information locally on your device only:
- Your game settings (e.g., grid size, rounds per test, learning aids toggles).
- Your progress (e.g., which categories you have completed).

This data is stored securely on your device using Apple's standard technologies. Neither we nor any third party can access this data. If you delete the App, this data is permanently deleted.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Data Collection
The App does not use any third-party services that collect data. Specifically, we do not use:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Crash reporting services
- Social media integrations
- Cloud synchronization

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, there are no privacy risks for children.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
